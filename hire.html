THE ROLE

We are looking for a Senior Frontend Engineer who is passionate about crafting extraordinary digital experiences. You will lead frontend architecture decisions, mentor junior engineers, and build performant, accessible applications that serve millions of users globally across 40+ countries.

Key Responsibilities

  • Architect and develop scalable, high-performance frontend systems using React.js and Next.js
  • Drive technical decisions on component libraries, state management, and API integration patterns
  • Collaborate closely with product, design, and backend teams in an agile environment
  • Champion code quality through reviews, testing strategies, CI/CD pipelines, and documentation
  • Mentor mid-level and junior engineers and contribute to technical hiring processes
  • Build accessible, SEO-optimized interfaces with pixel-perfect attention to design detail

Requirements

  • 3–5 years of professional frontend development experience in production environments
  • Expert proficiency in React.js, TypeScript, and Next.js with a strong portfolio
  • Solid understanding of REST APIs, state management (Redux/Zustand), and testing (Vitest/Jest)
  • Experience with Three.js, WebGL, or advanced CSS animations is a strong advantage
  • Excellent communication skills and a collaborative, growth-oriented mindset
